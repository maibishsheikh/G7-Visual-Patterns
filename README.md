# MosaicQuest — Grade 7 · Visual Patterns

**Interactive Mathematics Learning Module**  
Aligned with Singapore MOE Secondary 1 Mathematics curriculum (Number Patterns & Geometric Symmetry strands).

---

## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Architecture

MosaicQuest teaches visual and spatial pattern literacy:
- Spotting and extending repeating (cyclical) motifs.
- Distinguishing **repeating patterns** from **growing patterns**.
- Representing growing patterns through systematic counting (without premature algebra).
- Comparing constant (linear) growth against increasing (quadratic) growth.
- Continuing patterns generated by **rotation** (clockwise/anti-clockwise) and **reflection** (mirror line).
- Recognizing **line symmetry** and **rotational symmetry**.
- Predicting far figures through spatial reasoning.

### Five-Phase Journey
1. **Wonder** — *The Broken Panel Mystery*: 3 missing tiles in the workshop's historic panel.
2. **Story** — *The Apprentice Commission*: 4 widescreen story panels featuring Nadia, Arjun, and Kaleido the Chameleon 🦎.
3. **Simulate** — 4 Interactive Stations:
   - **Station 1: Kaleidoscope Wall Lab** (Concept Discovery Lab)
   - **Station 2: Match the Master's Pattern** (Build-to-Target Challenge)
   - **Station 3: Design the Mosaic Panel** (Multi-Step Construction & Far-Figure Prediction)
   - **Station 4: Spot the Broken Tile** (Error-Detective Lab)
4. **Practice** — 10 Themed Worlds, 100 procedural questions, 4 Practice Modes, and 10 Master Critique Boss Battles.
5. **Reflect** — 3 Misconception Recap Questions, Journal Reflection, Scorecard, and Master Artisan rank.

## 3. Art Brief — 4 Story Panels (PRD §13)

### Panel 1: The Broken Panel
- **Scene**: Historic mosaic restoration studio. An ancient, ornate geometric mosaic wall panel with three shattered gaps in the border.
- **Characters**: Nadia (curious, observant apprentice) with pencil and measuring caliper, and Arjun (methodical apprentice) inspecting the damaged tiles.
- **Lighting/Mood**: Warm workshop lantern light, dusty rose and terracotta tiles, rich craftsman atmosphere.

### Panel 2: Repeating or Growing?
- **Scene**: Craftsman design table covered with loose glazed tiles, drafting compasses, and pattern strips.
- **Characters**: Kaleido the Chameleon perched atop a stone tile, displaying color-shifting emerald and violet skin. Nadia and Arjun watching intently as Kaleido demonstrates a cycling tile strip vs. an expanding square.
- **Lighting/Mood**: Vibrant, playful, educational.

### Panel 3: Turn, Flip, and Match
- **Scene**: Close-up of the drafting board with a vertical dashed mirror line and a circular rotation protractor dial.
- **Characters**: Arjun turning an L-shaped mosaic tile 90° clockwise and then flipping it across the mirror line. Nadia observing that sliding the tile is not a true reflection.
- **Lighting/Mood**: Clean architectural drafting feel, crisp cobalt and gold jewel tiles.

### Panel 4: Restoring the Panel
- **Scene**: The restored mosaic panel gleaming in full glory on the exhibition pedestal, every gap filled with mathematically aligned tiles.
- **Characters**: Nadia and Arjun presenting the finished panel to the Workshop Master, who nods with approval. Kaleido perches proudly with celebration sparkles.
- **Lighting/Mood**: Warm celebration, master artisan rank achievement.
